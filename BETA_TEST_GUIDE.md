# Road Clock Beta 1 Test Guide

## Before the drive
- Open Road Clock from the installed home-screen app.
- Allow Precise Location.
- Calculate a familiar route.
- Confirm ETA, distance, and drive time appear.
- Expand the trip drawer and inspect the shutdown suggestion.

## During the drive
A passenger should record observations. The driver should not troubleshoot while moving.

Check:
- Does the arrow remain near the actual vehicle position?
- Does the camera glide without visible shaking?
- Does the heading match the road?
- Do maneuver distances count down sensibly?
- Does Road Clock recover after leaving and reopening the app?
- Does the Resume button restore follow mode?
- Does voice AI answer without freezing?

## Demo test
Calculate a route and tap **RUN JESSE DEMO DRIVE**. This validates the visual movement pipeline without real travel.

## Report format
- Phone model and operating-system version
- Installed app or browser tab
- Road type
- What happened
- Whether it happened once or repeatedly
- Screenshot when safe
